Questions

Will the posts sound like a robot wrote them?

No — you pick the tone (Friendly, Professional, Playful, Bold) and the AI writes in your business's voice, about your actual services. Owners edit less than you'd expect.

What if I run out of topics?

Leave the topic blank and the AI picks for you — promos, behind-the-scenes, tips, holidays, customer stories. It never repeats the same angle twice in a row.

How do I access it after subscribing?

You get a personal access key by email. Enter it once in the app — it's saved on your device, so you're in with one click from then on.

What happens when I cancel?

Your access key is revoked and billing stops. No lock-in, no fees.
